# Google Merchant Center Promotions Playbook

## Overview
This playbook provides steps to activate and manage promotions in Google Merchant Center, specifically focusing on the PRINT10 promotion.

## PRINT10 Promotion Workflow
1. **Prepare Your Merchant Center Account**
   - Ensure that your Merchant Center account is set up correctly.

2. **Create the PRINT10 Promotion**
   - Log in to Google Merchant Center.
   - Navigate to Promotions and click on "Add".
   - Fill in the promotion details including promotional code "PRINT10".

3. **Set Promotion Schedule**
   - Define start and end dates for the promotion.

4. **Review and Activate**
   - Review all details for accuracy.
   - Click "Activate" to go live with the promotion.
