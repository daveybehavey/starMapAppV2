# Constellation Creator

Welcome to the Constellation Creator! This tool allows you to explore and design your own constellations based on your unique preferences and creativity.

## What is a Constellation?

A constellation is a group of stars that form a recognizable pattern in the night sky. They have been used for navigation and storytelling throughout history.

## How to Use the Constellation Creator

1. **Select Your Stars**: Choose from a wide variety of stars available in our database.
2. **Create Your Pattern**: Use our interactive tool to drag and drop stars into your desired configuration.
3. **Save and Share**: Once you're satisfied with your creation, save it and share it with friends!
