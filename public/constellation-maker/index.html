Constellation Maker

Design custom star patterns, map constellations, and export high-resolution star chart images — built for educators, hobbyists, and designers.

About this tool

Our Constellation Maker helps you create meaningful star patterns by letting you place stars, connect them with lines, add labels, and adjust visual styles. Whether you're creating an art piece, teaching astronomy basics, or personalizing a gift, this tool makes it easy to produce beautiful, accurate star maps.

Key features

  • Interactive placement: click to add or move stars, drag to reposition.
  • Custom connections: draw lines between stars to form constellations.
  • Labels & annotations: add names, dates, and notes for each star.
  • Styling options: change star size, color, line thickness, and background.
  • Export & share: download high-resolution PNG/SVG or share a link to your map.

How to use

  1. Open the Constellation Maker interface.
  2. Click on the canvas to add stars. Drag to reposition as needed.
  3. Use the connect tool to draw lines between stars and form constellations.
  4. Add labels and adjust visual settings from the side panel.
  5. Export your final design in PNG or SVG format for print or web.
