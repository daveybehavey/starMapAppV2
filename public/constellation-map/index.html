Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the constellation map?
Our maps are generated from precise astronomical data and reflect star positions accurate to the date and time you provide.
Do I need to provide a specific date and time?
We recommend providing the exact date and time for the most accurate sky view. If time is unknown, we can use midnight of the selected date, which may slightly alter visible star positions.
What is the difference between framed and digital products?
Framed maps are printed and mounted, ready to hang. Digital maps are high-resolution image files suitable for printing yourself or sharing online.
