Why create a star map?

Creating a star map freezes a moment in the sky — the stars above a meaningful place and time. A custom star map turns astronomy into a personal keepsake that tells a story: the night you met, the moment of a birth, or any memory you want to remember forever.

How to create a star map (quick guide)

1. Choose a date and location.

Enter the exact date and time and pick the place (city, coordinates, or venue). We calculate the star chart for that moment in the sky.

2. Pick a design and size.

Select color schemes, constellation labels, and your preferred dimensions — from small prints to large framed posters.

3. Add a personal message.

Customize the title, subtitle, and any short message or location note to make the star map uniquely yours.

4. Preview and order.

Preview the star map, choose framing or materials, and complete your order. We provide print-ready quality with accurate astronomical data.

Our star map generator uses precise astronomical algorithms to recreate the sky for any location and moment. We plot visible stars, constellations, and the ecliptic so the result is both beautiful and scientifically accurate.

Frequently asked questions about creating a star map

Can I create a star map for any date?
Yes — past, present, or future. Provide the date and time and we calculate the sky for that exact moment.
How accurate is the location?
We accept city names, addresses, or latitude/longitude coordinates. The accuracy of the map depends on the location precision you provide.
Do you include the Moon and planets?
We can display the Moon and visible planets on the star map if you choose those options in the customization step.
