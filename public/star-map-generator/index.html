What does "create a star map" mean?

To create a star map is to generate an accurate view of the night sky for a specific date, time, and location. Our star map generator uses astronomical data to plot stars, constellations, and celestial landmarks so you can preserve a memory in a beautiful visual format.

Why people create a star map

  • Commemorate a special date (wedding, birth, anniversary).
  • Design a personalized, meaningful gift.
  • Turn a moment into wall art with accurate star positions.

How to create a star map — quick steps

  1. Choose the date and exact location for the moment you want to preserve.
  2. Select the time and timezone for the most accurate sky view.
  3. Pick a design: color scheme, labels, and format (print or digital).
  4. Preview and download or order a printed star map.

Example: To create a star map for July 20, 1969 in Cape Canaveral at 20:18 UTC — enter the date, location, and time and generate the map. The result is an accurate depiction of stars and constellations as they appeared above that place and time.

Tips for the best star map

  • Use the exact coordinates (latitude/longitude) when possible for maximum precision.
  • Decide whether to include constellation lines, planetary positions, or a horizon line.
  • Choose high-contrast color palettes for wall art to ensure visibility from a distance.
