# STARMAPCO Social Media Playbook

This document is the working playbook for launching and growing STARMAPCO on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Reels, and Pinterest.

The goal is not to "post on everything." The goal is to build one repeatable content engine that:

- grows the accounts
- drives profile visits and clicks
- gets people into the preview flow
- converts gift buyers

## 1. Positioning

### Core Brand Line

`We turn meaningful moments into beautiful gifts.`

### What STARMAPCO Actually Sells

STARMAPCO sells a personalized gift built from a meaningful date, time, and location.

Do not lead with:

- astronomy
- celestial decor
- constellation rendering
- software features

Lead with:

- the moment
- the story
- the gift
- the finished keepsake

### Core Offer Truths

- Users can choose any date, time, and location.
- Users can preview before purchasing.
- The final output can be digital, unframed print, or framed print.
- Physical print messaging should stay accurate to the live setup. If physical shipping is only in the U.S., say that on print-focused content.

## 2. Audience

### Primary Buyers

- Women buying thoughtful gifts for partners, parents, friends, and family
- Men buying anniversary, proposal, wedding, and Valentine's gifts
- Gift buyers searching for something personal, premium, and easy

### Best Occasion Angles

- anniversary
- proposal
- wedding
- new baby
- birthday
- memorial
- graduation
- Mother's Day
- Father's Day
- Christmas
- first home

### Buyer Psychology

The buyer is usually thinking:

- I need a gift
- I want it to feel personal
- I want it to look premium
- I do not want to overthink it

Your content should answer:

- Who is this for?
- What moment does it capture?
- What do I get?
- Will it look beautiful?
- How easy is it to make?

## 7. The Content Operating System

### The Core Rule

Organic content tests ideas.

Winning organic content becomes ad creative.

Do not build expensive ads in the dark if organic content can tell you what resonates first.

### Weekly Mix

- 50% occasion and gift-angle content
- 20% how-it-works content
- 15% proof and transformations
- 10% FAQ and objections
- 5% direct promotional pushes

### Daily Posting Baseline

- TikTok: 1 post per day
- Instagram Reels: 5 to 7 posts per week
- Facebook Reels: repost winners
- Pinterest: 5 to 10 pins per week
- Short-form total target: 7+ posts per week minimum across TikTok/Instagram/Facebook

### Hook Variant Testing Standard

For every high-priority video concept, test at least 3 opening hooks before deciding the winner.

Naming pattern:

- `VID-004-A`
- `VID-004-B`
- `VID-004-C`

Use the same base footage and change:

- hook line
- first-screen text
- first 1 to 2 seconds pacing

## 9. CapCut Production System

This is the default editing system for STARMAPCO. Keep it simple and repeatable.

### Project Structure

Create a new CapCut project for each batch:

- `Week01_Batch01`
- `Week01_Batch02`

Within each batch, keep videos named:

- `01_core-gift`
- `02_anniversary`
- `03_wedding`
- `04_new-baby`
- `05_last-minute`

### Default Video Specs

- aspect ratio: `9:16`
- target length: `15s` to `22s`
- fps: use the source fps; do not overcomplicate this early
- export resolution: `1080x1920`

### STARMAPCO Editing Rules

- show the finished product in the first second
- keep cuts every `1` to `1.5` seconds
- no long logo intro
- no generic galaxy filler footage
- every video must work with sound off
- use burned-in captions
- one video = one idea

### Recommended Text System

Pick one serif and one clean sans. Use them consistently.

If available in CapCut, use something like:

- serif: `Cormorant Garamond` or `Libre Baskerville`
- sans: `Montserrat` or `Poppins`

Use the serif for:

- hook screens
- emotional lines
- premium reveal shots

Use the sans for:

- steps
- subtitles
- CTA text

### Suggested Caption Style

- white or warm ivory text
- subtle shadow for readability
- keep text in the center-safe zone
- avoid placing text under TikTok/Instagram UI areas

### Caption Rules

- max 5 to 8 words per screen when possible
- punchy first line
- no full paragraphs on screen
- animate text lightly, not aggressively

### Default Timeline Recipe

#### Recipe A: Core Product Video

- `0.0s - 1.5s`: strongest finished map shot + hook
- `1.5s - 5.0s`: date/location entry on phone or screen
- `5.0s - 9.0s`: preview reveal and personalization
- `9.0s - 14.0s`: framed or digital proof shot
- `14.0s - 18.0s`: CTA

#### Recipe B: Occasion Video

- `0.0s - 1.5s`: finished product + occasion hook
- `1.5s - 4.5s`: enter date and location
- `4.5s - 8.0s`: add names or message
- `8.0s - 13.0s`: real room or print close-up
- `13.0s - 18.0s`: CTA

#### Recipe C: FAQ Video

- `0.0s - 1.5s`: question on screen
- `1.5s - 5.0s`: show relevant UI or product state
- `5.0s - 10.0s`: quick answer
- `10.0s - 15.0s`: final product proof
- `15.0s - 18.0s`: CTA

### Motion Rules

- use subtle zooms
- use fast cuts
- use simple fades or quick push transitions
- avoid overdesigned glitch effects
- avoid trend templates that make the product look cheap

### Audio Rules

- keep voiceover dry and clear
- music should support mood, not dominate
- use lower-volume romantic or calm background music
- prioritize understandable voiceover over song choice

### Voiceover Recording Tips

- record in a quiet room
- stand while reading if possible
- speak casually, not like a TV ad
- keep energy warm and direct
- trim long pauses

### B-Roll Checklist

Every batch should include:

- 3 wall shots
- 3 close-up detail shots
- 3 hands / gifting shots
- 5 editor screen recordings
- 3 digital preview shots

### Thumbnail Rules

Choose a cover frame with:

- finished map visible
- one clean readable hook
- no clutter

### Export Checklist

- no spelling mistakes
- hook visible in first frame
- captions readable on mobile
- CTA included
- no dead air at the end
- safe for sound-off viewing

## 16. Daily Workflow

1. Pick one video concept.
2. Choose and label the hook version (`A`, `B`, or `C`).
3. Pull the caption and hashtags.
4. Post to TikTok first.
5. Repost to Instagram Reels.
6. Repost winners to Facebook Reels.
7. Turn the same idea into one or two Pinterest pins.
8. Reply to comments for 15 to 20 minutes.
9. Save comments and questions as future content ideas.
10. Mark `promote_as_ad=yes` for organic winners worth paid testing.

## 17. Before Posting Checklist

- finished map visible in the first second
- hook readable immediately
- vertical `9:16`
- burned captions included
- one clear CTA
- short caption
- relevant hashtags only
- clean cover image
- no dead intro

## 18. After Posting Checklist

- reply to comments in the first hour
- note comments that reveal objections or confusion
- watch for profile visits
- watch for link clicks
- watch for preview starts
- remake winners with new hooks

## 19. What Counts As A Winner

A video is worth repeating if it gets:

- strong early retention
- good watch time
- profile visits
- link clicks
- preview starts
- comments like "this is such a good gift"

### If A Post Gets Views But Few Clicks

Improve:

- the CTA
- the clarity of the offer
- the product proof

### If A Post Gets Clicks But Weak Retention

Improve:

- the first second
- the hook
- the pacing

### If A Post Gets Both Attention And Clicks

Remake it in:

- 3 new hook variations
- 2 new captions
- 1 new occasion angle
