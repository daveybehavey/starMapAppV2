## Strategy: four lanes (run in parallel)

**Acquisition** and **offer** both move revenue. Do not treat catalog or shipping promos as “later” if they are **wired in checkout** and margin-checked.

### Lane 1 — Paid Search (wedding wedge)

**Status:** Playbook ready (`ADS_RELAUNCH_SETUP.md`); launch or explicitly defer.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|--------|
| Campaign | Search only · `gift_wedding_2026` |
| Budget | $10–15/day start |
| Landing | `/wedding` + full UTMs |
| Kill switch | 7d + ≥$70 spend + 0 GA4 purchases → pause |
| Target CPA | ≤$50 framed (margin ~$64 US) |

**Best practice:** Lead ads to **framed** offer; mention free preview + shipping honesty. Scale only after 2+ attributed purchases in 7 days.

### Lane 2 — Organic + AI (SEO / AIEO)

You already have infrastructure (money pages, FAQ schema, `/how-accurate-are-star-maps`). One **ChatGPT-attributed** framed sale proved demand.

| Action | Cadence | Owner |
|--------|---------|-------|
| GSC top queries → title/H1 on **one** ranking page (B5) | Weekly | 45 min |
| Answer-first copy on top 5 URLs (home, wedding, personalized, generator, accuracy) | Once, then tune | Half day |
| `llms.txt` at site root (canonical URLs + product facts) | Once | 1 hr |
| GA4 segment: source contains `chatgpt`, `perplexity`, `bing`, `copilot` | Once | 30 min |
| Review `marketing_sources` in `qa:commerce-digest` | Weekly | In scorecard |

**Best practice:** Question headings, FAQ blocks, accuracy/trust page linked from money pages. No fake reviews.

### Lane 3 — Compounding trust

| Action | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Ask happy buyers for permissioned quote (B4) | Lifts conversion on cold traffic |
| Pinterest / seasonal blog (Mother's Day, anniversary, Christmas) | Visual gift discovery |
| Promo list nurture (16 subs) | Tiny today; capture + 1 email/month |
| Merchant Center feed healthy | Shopping surface for product queries |

### Lane 4 — Offer & catalog (conversion + AOV)

**Not deferred** when each item passes margin + one real test order. Sequence matters; skipping the category does not.

| Initiative | Status in code | Margin / risk | Recommended move |
|------------|----------------|---------------|------------------|
| **Free shipping $100+** (merchandise subtotal) | Not wired — removed fake $150+ in 1A-1 | Framed ~$99 + HD ~$7 crosses $100; subsidize ~$10–13 ship → still ~$50+ profit US | **Do next** — checkout must **waive** shipping, not just marketing copy (Merchant Center + ads must match) |
| **Canvas gallery wrap** | In catalog; not on paywall | `launch_ready` (~$41 profit US @ $59) | **Pilot** after one test order; position between poster and framed |
| **Greeting card bundle** | Live on framed paywall | Bundle-only | Mention in wedding ads / money pages |
| **Mug** | In catalog; `/shop` path | `test_limited` | **Shop only** — not wedding hero |
| **Unframed poster hero push** | Live | Tight margin in some countries (`reprice_before_launch`) | Fix price/margin before pushing in ads |
| **Gift tiers UX (D1)** | Partial (paywall rows exist) | Mostly copy/UX | Clarify ladder: Digital → Poster → Framed (+ card) |

**Rule:** one catalog change per deploy + `qa:print-margin` + one paid proof order when the SKU is new to live checkout.

## What we defer (still)

These add **ops surface** or **trust risk** without proportional revenue at current traffic:

- **Multiple SKUs in one week** (canvas + mug + canvas on paywall together)
- **Margin guard blocking checkout** until promo/free-ship economics are modeled
- **Mobile app / RevenueCat**
- **Public `/order-status`** (nice; not a growth lever)
- **Broad blog spam**

**Not deferred:** real free-shipping threshold, canvas pilot, shop mug, card bundle marketing — see Lane 4.

## Decision rules

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| `qa:revenue-goal` pace &lt; 70% mid-month | Split week: **one acquisition** action + **one offer** action (e.g. free ship + GSC tune) |
| Ads CPA &gt; $64 on framed | Pause; fix landing or creative |
| AI/organic orders ≥ 2/month | Invest in B5 + `llms.txt` before scaling ads |
| Print ops `failed` &gt; 0 | Pause ads until ops green |
| Q4 week with 0 sales | Emergency: raise budget on proven keyword only |
