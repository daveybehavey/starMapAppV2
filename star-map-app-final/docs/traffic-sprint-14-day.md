## 1) Non-Negotiable Operating Rules

1. One post = one idea.
2. Hook appears in the first second.
3. Product is understandable in three seconds.
4. Every post points to one matching landing page.
5. Every post uses tracked links (UTM + platform source).
6. Review results daily and cut losers quickly.

## 2) Daily Output Targets

- Short-form videos: `2` per day (TikTok first, repost to Instagram Reels, optional Facebook Reels).
- Pinterest: `2` fresh pins per day after setup day.
- Referral social posts: minimum `3` per week from `/success` or `/download` share surfaces.

## 3) Approved Pillars

1. Moment hook (the night we met, wedding day, baby born).
2. Product reveal (date + place -> map result).
3. Gift angle (anniversary, wedding, Mother's Day, last-minute).
4. Aesthetic/proof (clean premium result, framed/unframed clarity).

## 4) Creative Specs

- Vertical `9:16`, `1080x1920`, target `6-12s`.
- Clean readable text, one font family, no clutter.
- Cuts every `0.5-1.5s`.
- Keep transitions simple (`cut`, `fade`, light zoom).

## 5) Link + Destination Discipline

Use one primary destination per post:

- Anniversary: `/anniversary`
- Wedding: `/wedding`
- Birthday: `/birthday`
- Gift-general: `/star-map-gift`
- Last-minute digital: `/star-map-generator`
- Night sky wall-art angle: `/night-sky-map-gift`

UTM standard for organic social:

- `utm_source`: `tiktok`, `instagram`, `facebook`, `pinterest`
- `utm_medium`: `organic_social`
- `utm_campaign`: `traffic_sprint_apr2026`
- `utm_content`: post ID (example `VID-021-A`)

Referral social posts keep using the referral standard from `docs/social-referral-campaign-playbook.md`.

## 6) First 10 Video Queue (Execution Order)

1. Core concept: "This is what the sky looked like when we met"
2. Anniversary angle: "Best anniversary gift if you actually want them to cry"
3. POV gift: "POV: you stopped giving boring gifts"
4. Wedding angle: "Your wedding night sky, turned into wall art"
5. Curiosity: "I didn't know you could print the exact stars from a specific date"
6. Style choice: "Which style would you choose?"
7. Emotional line: "The night our whole life changed"
8. Mother's Day/family: "The day you became a mom, turned into art"
9. Product clarity: "So what is a star map?"
10. Direct conversion: "If you have an important date, this is for you"

## 7) Day-by-Day Checklist

### Day 1

1. Record one clean screen-capture batch (date entry, location entry, style switch, final map).
2. Publish video 1 and video 2.
3. Repost both to Instagram Reels.
4. Log all rows in `traffic-sprint-tracker.csv`.

### Day 2

1. Publish video 5 and video 6.
2. Repost both to Instagram Reels.
3. Publish two matching Pinterest pins.
4. Update metrics for day 1 posts.

### Day 3

1. Publish video 4 and video 7.
2. Repost both to Instagram Reels.
3. Publish two Pinterest pins.
4. Mark first winner/loser calls from days 1-2 data.

### Days 4-7

1. Publish two short-form posts daily from remaining queue + first remakes.
2. Publish two fresh Pinterest pins daily.
3. Add one referral social post.
4. Produce three A/B hook variants for the top winner.

### Days 8-10

1. Keep two short-form posts daily.
2. Keep two Pinterest pins daily.
3. Add Mother's Day and graduation angles.
4. Add one FAQ video from real comments/DMs.

### Days 11-14

1. Keep daily volume.
2. Run hard winner/loser decisions.
3. Promote only proven winners into ad test candidates.
4. Build next 14-day queue from winning patterns only.

## 8) Kill/Scale Rules

Evaluate every post at `24h` and `72h`.

Winner (scale):

- Top-tier `preview_starts_per_1k_views` for the week.
- `avg_watch_percent` at or above week median.
- Clear audience signal (saves/shares/comments) or direct checkout movement.

Loser (kill):

- Bottom tier `preview_starts_per_1k_views` at `72h`.
- Weak watch and no downstream movement.
- Do not remake. Replace hook + opening frame instead.

## 9) Weekly Review Output

At end of day 7 and day 14:

1. Complete `docs/weekly-social-review.md`.
2. Pick top 3 winners and produce 2 remakes each.
3. Archive bottom 30% concepts.
4. Carry forward only hooks and pillars with real conversion signals.

## 10) Guardrails

1. Do not ship fake testimonials or unapproved proof.
2. Do not increase paid spend on unproven organic concepts.
3. Do not break checkout/message consistency to chase clicks.
4. Keep product claims accurate to actual generator output.
