# STARMAPCO Filming Day Checklist

Use this for one focused batch-filming day that produces enough footage for at least 5 short-form videos and a reusable shot bank.

The goal is not to leave with 5 perfectly edited videos. The goal is to leave with enough raw material to make 10 to 15 strong edits.

## 1. Filming Day Outcome

By the end of the session, you should have:

- 5 hero finished-map shots
- 5 to 10 editor screen recordings
- 5 print close-up detail shots
- 3 to 5 framed wall shots
- 3 to 5 hands or gift-handoff shots
- 3 occasion-specific variations
- 5 to 10 voiceover takes

## 2. The Day Before

### Pick The Concepts

Choose the 5 videos you are filming.

Recommended starter set:

1. core gift
2. anniversary
3. wedding
4. new baby
5. last-minute gift

### Pick The Hooks

Lock one hook per video before filming. Do not improvise everything on set.

### Export The Maps

Prepare finished examples in advance for:

- anniversary
- wedding
- new baby
- memorial
- birthday

### Gather Props

- framed print
- unframed print
- digital mockup on phone or laptop
- envelope, ribbon, or gift wrap
- clean desk or table props
- warm home decor props if available

### Prep The Room

- clean the background
- remove clutter
- choose one bright area and one cozy area
- test wall shots before the session starts

## 3. Gear Checklist

### Required

- phone with good camera
- tripod or stable stand
- charger or power bank
- microfiber cloth for the lens
- headphones for checking audio

### Helpful

- small light or ring light
- simple clip mic
- second phone for playback or reference
- extension cable

## 4. Creative Rules

- shoot everything vertical `9:16`
- show the finished product early
- keep clips short
- shoot more takes than you think you need
- get both steady shots and light handheld shots
- avoid cluttered backgrounds
- avoid generic space stock footage
- keep the product looking premium and real

## 5. Shot Order

Shoot in this order so you do not waste time.

### 1. Hero Product Shots

Capture:

- framed print on wall
- framed print on desk or shelf
- slow push-in on finished print
- clean close-up of the center detail

### 2. Close-Up Proof Shots

Capture:

- print texture
- frame corner
- text detail
- names and date line
- full map close-up

### 3. Hands And Gift Shots

Capture:

- hands holding print
- unwrapping or opening
- setting frame on table
- pointing to custom date or names

### 4. Editor And Phone Shots

Capture:

- entering the date
- entering the location
- selecting style
- editing the title
- editing the dedication line
- preview loading
- final preview on screen

### 5. Occasion Variants

Capture one short sequence for each:

- anniversary
- wedding
- new baby
- hard-to-shop-for gift
- last-minute gift

### 6. Voiceover Pickups

Record:

- 2 takes per script
- 1 slower take
- 1 more natural take

## 6. Must-Have Shot Bank

Do not end the day without these.

- finished framed wall shot
- finished unframed tabletop shot
- close-up of names and date
- phone entering date and location
- map preview reveal
- hands holding the print
- gift wrap or envelope shot
- room reveal with framed print

## 7. First 5 Video Capture Lists

### Core Gift

Capture:

- best framed reveal
- entering date and location
- preview loading
- editing names
- final wall shot

### Anniversary

Capture:

- romantic finished print
- anniversary date entry
- couple-style text line
- framed close-up

### Wedding

Capture:

- elegant finished print
- proposal or wedding date entry
- custom names line
- home wall reveal

### New Baby

Capture:

- soft finished print
- birth date and time entry
- baby name line
- nursery-style room shot

### Last-Minute Gift

Capture:

- quick opening "gift panic" style setup
- fast editor flow
- digital preview
- print option proof

## 8. Clip Framing Rules

- keep the product centered enough for text overlays
- leave space at the top for the hook
- leave space at the bottom for captions and CTA
- keep TikTok and Instagram UI safe zones in mind

## 9. Audio Rules

- record room tone for a few seconds
- avoid HVAC or fan noise if possible
- record voiceovers in a quiet room after filming if needed
- prioritize clarity over "radio voice"

## 10. On-Set Efficiency Rules

- film in batches by shot type, not by final video
- do not stop to edit during filming
- rename or favorite the best takes immediately
- keep the phone lens clean
- review key clips before changing locations

## 11. End-Of-Day Wrap

Before finishing, make sure you have:

- enough clips for all 5 planned videos
- at least 3 backup clips for each concept
- the best hero shot favorited
- voiceover files saved
- props packed and prints protected

## 12. Upload And Organize

Sort clips immediately into:

- `raw-screen-recordings`
- `raw-lifestyle`
- `finished-prints`
- `voiceovers`
- `best-takes`

Rename standout clips clearly, for example:

- `hero-wall-anniversary-01`
- `phone-date-entry-02`
- `new-baby-text-closeup-01`

## 13. Common Mistakes

- spending too long on one concept
- not shooting enough close-ups
- forgetting clean CTA end shots
- filming everything too wide
- filming only polished shots and no native-feeling footage
- leaving without enough editor or phone footage

## 14. Minimum Success Standard

If the day feels messy but you leave with:

- 40 to 60 usable raw clips
- 5 strong hero shots
- 5 clean phone/editor clips
- 3 solid room or print proof shots

the filming day was productive.
