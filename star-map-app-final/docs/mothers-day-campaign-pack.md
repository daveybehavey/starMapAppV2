# STARMAPCO Mother's Day Campaign Pack

This campaign pack is for the U.S. Mother's Day window ending on `Sunday, May 10, 2026`.

Use this pack to plan and execute Mother's Day content, emails, Pinterest, and paid creative without inventing the strategy from scratch.

## 1. Campaign Goal

Drive gift buyers into the STARMAPCO preview flow for Mother's Day gifting, then convert them into digital, unframed, or framed purchases depending on timing and buyer needs.

## 2. Core Positioning

Mother's Day is not about "space art."

It is about:

- the day she became a mom
- a child's birth date
- a family milestone
- a shared memory worth keeping

Primary message:

`Give mom the sky from a moment that changed your family.`

## 7. Timing Plan

### Phase 1: Warm-Up

`March 10, 2026` to `April 5, 2026`

Goal:

- introduce the Mother's Day angle early
- gather engagement and saves
- test which hooks resonate

Content types:

- first Mother's Day idea videos
- family milestone angle
- "what date should I choose for mom?" FAQ
- room-reveal and print-proof videos

### Phase 2: Consideration

`April 6, 2026` to `April 26, 2026`

Goal:

- make STARMAPCO feel like a serious Mother's Day option
- drive preview starts
- increase frequency

Content types:

- Mother's Day gift guides
- first Mother's Day keepsake
- gift for wife from husband
- gift for mom from daughter / son
- framed vs digital choice content

### Phase 3: Urgency

`April 27, 2026` to `May 6, 2026`

Goal:

- convert people who are deciding now
- push preview starts hard
- emphasize gift-readiness and emotional payoff

Content types:

- "still need a Mother's Day gift?"
- "last-minute, still personal"
- print-ready and gift-ready proof
- strongest emotional ads

### Phase 4: Final Days

`May 7, 2026` to `May 10, 2026`

Goal:

- capture late buyers
- shift harder toward digital and preview-first framing if print timing is tight

Content types:

- last-minute meaningful gift
- digital gift option
- Mother's Day countdown
- FAQ replies and objection handling

Do not promise shipping cutoffs unless they are confirmed operationally.

## 17. Comment And DM Prompts

These questions are useful for engagement and for learning buyer language.

- `What date would you use for mom?`
- `Would you choose the birth date or another family milestone?`
- `Would you gift this framed or digital?`
- `Is this for your mom or your wife?`

## 18. Risks To Avoid

- leaning too hard into generic "mom deserves the stars" copy
- forgetting the family-date angle
- making everything about the product and not the moment
- promising shipping timing without confirmation
- waiting until late April to begin posting

## 19. Simple KPI Plan

- preview starts from first Mother's Day and wife-focused content
