# STARMAPCO Social Reply Bank

Use this document for public comments, DMs, and quick customer questions that come in from TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest.

The goal is to:

- respond fast
- sound human
- answer clearly
- move interested people toward the preview flow
- avoid sounding like a bot

## 1. Reply Rules

### Sound Like This

- warm
- simple
- direct
- tasteful
- helpful

### Do Not Sound Like This

- pushy
- robotic
- overly salesy
- corporate
- vague

### Default Response Structure

Use this pattern:

1. answer the question
2. reduce friction
3. point them to the next step

Example:

`Yes, you can use any date and location. Most people start with the moment that matters most, then preview it free before deciding.`

## 2. Quick CTA Lines

## 3. Public Comment Replies

`It is a custom star map made from a real date, time, and location. People usually use it for anniversaries, weddings, new babies, and other meaningful moments.`

`Yes. It is generated from the real sky for the date and location you choose.`

`Yes. You can use the location that matches the moment you want to remember.`

`Exact time helps, but it is not required to get started. The date and location are usually enough to create something beautiful.`

### "Can I preview it first?"

`Yes. You can preview the map before you decide what version you want.`

### "Can I add names or text?"

`Yes. You can add names, dates, and a short dedication line to make it feel personal.`

### "Do you have prints?"

`Yes. You can create the design first, then choose the format that fits how you want to gift it.`

### "Do you have framed prints?"

`Yes, framed is one of the options after you create the design.`

### "How long does it take?"

`The preview is quick. Most people can create the map itself in just a few minutes.`

### "This is such a good gift"

`That is exactly the goal. The right date does most of the work.`

### "This would be perfect for my anniversary"

`Anniversary is one of the best uses for it. The night you met or your wedding date usually works beautifully.`

### "What date should I choose?"

`Use the moment that changed everything. The night you met, the proposal, the wedding, a birth, or another date that still means something every time you think about it.`

### "Where do I buy?"

`You can start by previewing it free and building it from your date and location first.`

### "Do you ship?"

`Physical availability depends on the current print setup, but you can always start by previewing the design first.`

### "Do you ship to [country]?"

`I would check the current print availability at checkout for the physical version. You can still start by previewing the design first either way.`

### "How much is it?"

`The easiest move is to build the design first and then choose the version you want from there.`

If you want to be more direct and pricing is stable:

`There are digital and print options depending on how you want to gift it.`

### "Can this be a memorial gift?"

`Yes. Many people use a meaningful date and keep the design simple and quiet.`

## 8. Objection Handling

### "I do not know the exact time"

`That is okay. If you know the date and location, you can still start there.`

### "I am not sure what date to choose"

`Pick the moment that feels the most emotionally important. That usually leads to the best version.`

### "I want it to feel personal"

`That is why the date matters so much. The personalization starts with the moment, then the names and text make it even more specific.`

### "I need a gift that does not feel generic"

`That is exactly where this works best. It starts from a real memory, so it usually lands very differently than a generic gift.`

### "I am worried it will not look good"

`The best move is to preview it first. That lets you see how the date, layout, and text feel before deciding.`

- `the moment that changed everything`

- `the memory behind the gift`

## 14. Things To Avoid Saying

- anything that overpromises shipping timing
- anything that overstates availability if you are not sure
- anything that sounds defensive
- anything that sounds copied and pasted word-for-word every time

## 15. Best Practice

When possible:

- answer the question
- give one helpful suggestion
- softly point them toward the preview

That is usually enough to convert interest without sounding pushy.
