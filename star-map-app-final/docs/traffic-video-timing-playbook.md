## Fast editing checklist (every video)

1. Confirm hook appears in first second.
2. Confirm product is understandable by second 3.
3. Confirm emotional/giftable payoff before final second.
4. Keep total runtime under 12s unless performance data proves longer works.
5. Export and publish; do not over-polish a single post.
