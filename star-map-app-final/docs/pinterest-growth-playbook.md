# STARMAPCO Pinterest Growth Playbook

This is the execution doc for growing Pinterest traffic and purchases from gift-intent users.

Use this with:

- `social-command-center.md`
- `pinterest-content-tracker.csv`
- `weekly-social-review.md`

## 1. Pinterest Role In Your Funnel

Pinterest is not your main short-form testing channel. It is your search + shopping channel.

Primary job:

- get outbound clicks to gift-focused pages
- get saves on occasion-based pins
- turn winning pins into paid Pinterest ads later

Primary audience:

- women researching gifts
- partners searching anniversary / wedding / new baby gifts
- seasonal gift buyers (Mother's Day, graduation, holidays)

## 2. Day 1 Setup Checklist (Non-Negotiable)

1. Confirm Pinterest business account is active.
2. Claim `starmapco.com` in Pinterest settings.
3. Add website URL to profile and verify profile branding.
4. Install Pinterest Tag for conversion tracking.
5. Confirm `Conversion insights` is available in your account.
6. Create core boards (section 3 below).
7. Set board covers + keyworded board descriptions.
8. Enable pin scheduling for the next 14 days.

If any item above is incomplete, do that before designing more pins.

## 4. Pin Creative System

For each winning social concept (`VID-xxx`), create 3 Pinterest assets:

1. Video pin (`9:16`, 6 to 15 seconds) showing final product in first second.
2. Static pin (`2:3`) with one clear headline and one product visual.
3. Alternate static pin (`2:3`) with different hook text (A/B hook test).

Creative rules:

- one pin = one occasion angle
- text must be readable on mobile at a glance
- always show the finished map or framed print early
- avoid generic galaxy stock visuals that do not match your product output
- keep branding present but secondary to gift intent

## 5. Keyword + Metadata Workflow

Do this every Monday:

1. Use Pinterest Trends (United States, last 12 months).
2. Compare 3 to 4 terms per occasion (example: `anniversary gift`, `personalized anniversary gift`, `custom gift for wife`, `night sky map`).
3. Pick 10 to 15 priority terms for the week.
4. Build pin titles around those terms naturally.

Title formula:

`[occasion] + [personalized/custom] + [gift idea]`

Examples:

- Personalized anniversary gift from your wedding date
- Custom night sky map gift for new parents
- Last-minute meaningful gift with same-day digital delivery

Description formula:

1. problem/intent sentence
2. product sentence (what STARMAPCO creates)
3. CTA sentence with destination intent

Example:

`Looking for a meaningful anniversary gift? Create a custom star map from any date, time, and location, then choose digital or framed print. Preview your map now.`

## 6. 14-Day Execution Plan

### Days 1-2

1. finish setup checklist
2. create all core boards
3. prepare 6 pin templates in Canva/CapCut
4. select 5 landing pages to prioritize

### Days 3-4

1. create first 12 fresh pins (4 concepts x 3 assets each)
2. write titles/descriptions
3. assign each pin to the best matching board
4. schedule 2 fresh pins/day

### Days 5-7

1. publish daily
2. check analytics each day for saves and outbound clicks
3. duplicate top-performing pin and swap hook text (A/B)

### Days 8-10

1. create second batch of 12 fresh pins
2. add Mother's Day + graduation keyword angles
3. reuse best TikTok/Reels concept as Pinterest video pin

### Days 11-14

1. review top 10 pins by outbound clicks
2. mark 2 pins to test as paid ads (`promote_as_ad = yes`)
3. cut low-signal styles and keep only winning templates
4. plan next 14-day batch from winner patterns

## 7. Volume Target

Start with:

- 14 fresh pins in first 7 days
- 14 to 21 fresh pins in days 8 to 14

Minimum steady state after week 2:

- 2 fresh pins per day
- 1 repin or board refresh action per day

## 8. What To Track Weekly

Track these per pin:

- impressions
- saves
- pin clicks
- outbound clicks
- outbound click rate
- destination URL
- board
- hook version
- whether it should be promoted as ad

Decision rule:

- if a pin gets strong saves but weak outbound clicks, improve CTA and destination relevance
- if a pin gets strong outbound clicks, create 2 new variants and consider paid promotion

## 9. CapCut + Canva Build Recipe

Use this simple production flow:

1. pick one `VID` concept
2. export 6 to 12 second hero cut in CapCut
3. open Canva `1000x1500` template
4. place still frame or product mockup
5. add hook text (max 8 to 10 words)
6. export 2 hook variants (`A` and `B`)
7. upload and schedule in Pinterest

Name convention:

- `PIN-001-A-anniversary-static`
- `PIN-001-B-anniversary-static`
- `PIN-001-C-anniversary-video`

## 10. Common Mistakes To Avoid

- posting only one board and one keyword pattern
- pinning without a clear landing page match
- copying TikTok captions directly without search intent language
- overdesigning graphics with too much text
- changing style every day and never building repeatable templates
