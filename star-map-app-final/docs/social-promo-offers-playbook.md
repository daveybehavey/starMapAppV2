## Channel strategy

### Reddit

- Do not lead with obvious ad copy.
- Start with useful or story-led posts.
- Only use direct offers where the community rules allow it.
- If an organic post lands well, that is the one to amplify with paid promotion.

Reddit’s own business guidance favors promoting organic winners rather than leading with generic self-promo:
- https://www.business.reddit.com/learning-hub/articles/promote-organic-reddit-posts

### TikTok / Instagram

- Cleaner fit for explicit offer framing.
- Use the offer as a closer, not the hook.
- Hook with the moment or result first.

## First post angles

1. `If you already know the date, the hard part of the gift is done.`
2. `Still need a meaningful gift? Start with the exact night sky.`
3. `This is what the sky looked like when everything changed.`
4. `Last-minute gift. Still personal.`
5. `A 5-minute personalized gift that does not feel rushed.`

## Best-fit communities and channels

### Consumer communities to inspect first

- `r/weddingplanning`
- `r/Gifts`
- `r/LongDistance`
- `r/Mommit`
- `r/NewParents`

### Founder / distribution communities

- `r/SideProject`
- `r/smallbusiness`
- `r/Entrepreneur`

These founder/business communities are better for build-in-public or founder-story posts than direct gift-buyer acquisition.

### Rule note already verified

`r/weddingplanning` currently says discounts and deals belong in the `Daily Chat & Quick Questions` thread, not standalone posts.

## Guardrails

1. do not spray promo links across random subreddits
2. do not use one universal code for every channel
3. do not lead with the discount before the product story
4. do not scale paid social until one organic framing actually works
